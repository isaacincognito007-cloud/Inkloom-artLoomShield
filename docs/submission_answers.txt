- Tier 1 Deterministic regex, terminology dictionary, WCAG 2.1 AA, and CIE Delta-E color engine.

- Brand guidelines setup chip ("Set up for: ...") with "Redo setup" wizard shortcut.


TECHNOLOGY AND IMPLEMENTATION DETAILS
Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS on Vercel paired with Python 3.11/FastAPI and SQLite on Render, running deterministic rules and embedded scikit-learn TF-IDF fact retrieval on-device with zero external AI API keys. The hardest challenge was mapping and diffing character-level findings across varied file structures (PDF, DOCX, HTML) without corrupting underlying document markup.


WHAT CHANGED FROM YOUR ORIGINAL PROPOSAL, AND WHY?
We isolated preset setups into dedicated brand profiles instead of overwriting existing guidelines to prevent data loss, and enforced an honest 5 MB upload cap (deferring 100 MB streaming and OCR to Round 3) to guarantee stability on free-tier hosting.


WHAT DOES NOT WORK YET?
Render's free tier spins down on idle and takes ~60 seconds to wake on cold start, flat image text lacks OCR parsing, and scanned PDFs without selectable text streams are flagged as "Couldn't check".


YOUR THREE IMPROVEMENTS FOR ROUND 3

Improvement 1: Guideline Clarity Rewriter
An ambiguity optimizer that analyzes reviewer overrides and proposes deterministic rule rewrites for admin approval, eliminating subjective disputes and turning vague rules into consistent automated checks.

Improvement 2: Visual 4x4 Clear-Space & Accessibility Canvas Heatmap
A computer-vision canvas scanner that detects logos, measures clear-space exclusion perimeters, and renders a visual contrast heatmap, giving designers visual bounding boxes on their artwork instead of text error codes.

Improvement 3: Cryptographically Signed Compliance Certificate
A tamper-evident certificate generator that signs content SHA-256 hashes and Brand guidelines versions with an asymmetric private key, giving enterprises verifiable proof that published content was formally approved.
