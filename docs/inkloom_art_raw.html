How Inkloom will work

Four stages, each handled by a model built for that job. This is the pipeline in development — nothing here is running for users yet.

  1. 1

    Describe the business

    Name, sector, what it does, who it serves, and the character you want. Plain language, no prompt engineering.

  2. 2

    The brand model reads it

    A brand-analysis model turns that into concrete constraints: weight, width, geometry, counter shapes, and the symbol families that fit.

  3. 3

    Type and symbol models run

    A typography model sets the wordmark; a symbol model constructs the mark. They run against those constraints, not against a general aesthetic.

  4. 4

    The composition engine locks it up

    Optical alignment, clear space, and the lockup variants a real brand needs — horizontal, stacked, and the mark on its own.

Four systems, not one prompt

A general image model draws something logo-shaped. A logo is a system: it has to survive one colour, one inch, and one hundred applications.

Typography systems

Weight, width, contrast, optical sizing and the spacing rules that make a wordmark hold together at 16px and at 16 metres.

Symbol libraries

Geometric primitives and the construction rules for combining them — tangency, shared radii, consistent stroke terminals.

Composition engine

Optical alignment rather than mathematical centring, clear-space ratios, and every lockup a brand actually needs.

Brand analysis

Turns a description of a business into design constraints, so the output is reasoned about rather than sampled.

Questions people ask

Can Inkloom generate a logo right now?+

No. The models are in development. You can create an account, join early access and reserve credits today; generation is not enabled yet, and we will email you when it opens.

What is a credit?+

A credit is the unit Inkloom will charge for generation work. One generation will cost a set number of credits. Because generation is not live, nothing consumes credits yet.

Do early-access credits expire?+

No. Credits granted during early access do not expire. If that ever changes we will tell you before it applies, and it will not apply retroactively to credits already granted.

Will I have to pay at launch?+

Not to use your early-access credits. They are yours. Paid plans will exist alongside them, and early-access members get launch pricing.

Who owns the logos Inkloom makes?+

You will. Full commercial rights to the marks generated on your account, with no attribution requirement. The details will be in the terms before generation opens.

How is this different from asking an image model for a logo?+

A general image model produces a picture of a logo — often beautiful, usually unusable: soft edges, unrepeatable, no vector structure, text that is nearly right. Inkloom builds marks from typographic and geometric systems, so what comes out is constructed rather than drawn.
